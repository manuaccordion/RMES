RMES Revenue Manu&Enis System · configuration

—
Property configuration
Synced with the global property selector above. Weights/thresholds/compset are independent per property.

① Weights of the 5 RMES factors property: —

How much each factor contributes to the final multiplier. All 5 factors are computed at property level (Room Type differences come entirely from Foundation, via monthly historical supplements). Default: 20% × 5 fattori = 100%. Sum normalized automatically.

② RMES total cap property: —

Linear system: the RMES price = source × (1 + Σ weight × deviation). Each individual deviation is clamped to ±50%, the total sum is limited by the Cap below. Default 30%: the final price won't deviate by more than 30% from Foundation, even if all 5 factors point in the same direction.
Example: 25% means the final price will always stay within [source × 0.75, source × 1.25]. Press "Apply changes" below to save.

⚡ Foundation Pricing active

Foundation is the source of the RMES price. For each day and each RT, I compute a structural price from: LY history × target growth × pace × time curve × compset market cap × floor/base lift. The final RMES price is Foundation × (1 + Σ weights × deviations of the 5 factors). The old cascade (actual Beddy → My Expedia → Compset → ADR OTB → ADR Final LY) is now fallback only for days where Foundation cannot compute a price (e.g. no LY same-DOW same-month data available).
Changes to weights and thresholds are local: they are saved and applied to the current property only when you click the button on the right. Switch property above to edit the others.

🧱 Architecture & mobile app — how the files fit together

This dashboard is no longer a single standalone file. It is split into three files that live in the same folder and load together:

  • data.js — the data only (bookings CSV + Expedia rate-shopper data). This is the only file that changes when new bookings arrive; it is regenerated on each data update.
  • engine.js — the whole RMES engine: config, data loading, the 6-step Foundation, the 5 factors, weighted C·Pace, and every calculation and rendering function. This changes only when the pricing logic or the dashboard structure changes.
  • index.html — this page: markup, styles and the boot script. It pulls in data.js then engine.js before running.

A second page, app.html, is a phone-first companion that loads the same data.js and the same engine.js. Because the mobile app reuses the exact same engine, the RMES price it shows for a day is always identical to the dashboard's — they cannot drift apart. The mobile app is styled with an orange theme throughout (the same orange for every property, rather than the per-property colours). It has two tabs:

  • Daily Price — for each of the next 7 days the card shows, above the price, the day's occupancy OTB vs STLY with a coloured arrow (green = filling better than last year); a compact Price vs comp block with your Expedia price and the compset side by side (green gap = you are lower / more competitive); and a fixed two-column meta line (always shown) with € rank (price position, 1 = cheapest) and p/u 7d (last-7-days pickup delta vs STLY, coloured). Weekend nights (Fri/Sat) are marked by the weekday colour, not a background tint. Calendar on top to jump up to 365 days ahead. Tapping a day opens a sheet with: the Expedia price (and your Beddy RMES price), an Expedia vs compset block (your price, compset average, position, market position N of M, competitor range), on-the-books vs STLY, and the room categories ordered by price (lowest first, base room ★). It also has a price override for that day's base room, available two ways: a pencil icon ✏️ on each card in the 7-day list (tap it — not the card — to open an inline price field right there, Save/Reset; the card stays tappable for the full drill-in), and inside the drill-in itself. Either way the price is entered in Beddy space and stored in localStorage, per-device — it does not sync with the dashboard or other phones. The override immediately updates the Expedia price, the market position and the Rate Shopper pyramid, and shows a revenue simulation using the exact same model as the dashboard RMES modal (ΔRN% = −Δprice% × elasticity, elasticity read from localStorage, default 1.0). A second tap ("⚡ Show Foundation & RMES detail") expands the full breakdown: the Foundation 6 steps and the 5 RMES factors with each multiplier and the combined capped multiplier.
  • Monthly — a toggle at the top switches the whole view between OTB (on-the-books, compared vs STLY) and Forecast (projected close, compared vs Final LY); OTB is the default. A year-total card on top sums the year in the active view (a mix of closed months and OTB/forecast for future months, noted on the card). Then one card per month, default current month → December, with the three KPIs Revenue, ADR, Occupancy stacked, each showing the value and its variance vs the comparison. Past months are hidden behind a "Show past months" drill. The current month is highlighted, and each month card is tinted by revenue vs the comparison (light green = above, white = equal, light red = below) for an at-a-glance read.
  • Rate Shopper — a pyramid chart for the 7 visible days: your Expedia price (solid orange line), the compset average (dashed grey) and the competitor range as an orange min–max band, exactly like the dashboard Rate Shopper pyramid. Days without Expedia data are left blank.

It covers the four single properties; an aggregate "All" view is intentionally not offered because an aggregated RMES price is not meaningful.

When updating data: only data.js is rewritten, and a single publish updates both the dashboard and the mobile app. When changing logic: engine.js is rewritten and both pages pick it up automatically. The header line shows the live booking count so a successful data load is visible at a glance.

Reading the table (left → right)

One row = one future day. The column order is: Date → OTB → Pickup → STLY → Pickup STLY → Expedia Market → RMES (the Foundation column is hidden — click an RMES cell to see its full Foundation build-up).

  1. Date · Event · DoW — the day, the day of week (weekends coloured), any configured events.
  2. OTB to date — RN sold, OCC %, average ADR. The snapshot of right now. Click the RN to see every booking already on the books for that night.
  3. Pickup Ndays — recent trend: new, cancelled, ΔRN, new ADR. Configurable at 1/3/7/14/28 days. New and Cancelled are clickable for the list.
  4. STLY (−364) — where you stood at the same lead time last year. Day-of-week comparison (if today is Friday, it shows the Friday 52 weeks ago). Click the RN to see the bookings that were on the books that night a year ago.
  5. Pickup STLY — pickup over the same Ndays a year ago. Compare the 2 pickup columns to see whether you're doing better.
  6. Expedia Market (Mine · Compset) — across all properties. Mine = my Expedia price (Expedia converted to Beddy_eq by removing the OTA markup (configurable, not a fixed %) and the margin. (The separate Foundation column is hidden for clarity — its calculation now lives inside this RMES cell.)

Per-Room-Type price architecture (important)

The system works on 2 levels: the baseRT has a full calculation, the other RTs inherit.

Foundation: only the baseRT is actually computed. Each property has a baseRT (the room type with the most units, most historical data, most stable):

  • Firenze Suite → Camera Matrim. Deluxe (4 units of 8)
  • Condotta 16 → Bilocale (3 units of 7)
  • Palazzo Alfani → Classic (3 units of 9, from 01/02/2025)
  • Florence David's → Senape (1 apartment of 3)

For the baseRT, Foundation uses the full 6 steps. For non-base RTs (Suite, Suite with Terrace, Trilocale, Attico, Superior, Junior Suite, Deluxe), Foundation is NOT recomputed from the 6 steps; the shortcut is used:

Foundation_RT = Foundation_baseRT + RT_month_supplement

The monthly supplement is derived from history (average RT ADR − average baseRT ADR, split by high/low season, rounded to the nearest multiple of 5). Full table in tab RMES → Ⓔ RT supplements by month.

The 5 RMES factors are property-level, not per RT. The multiplier (e.g. ×0.767) is exactly the same for all RTs of that property on that day. Only the base price changes:

RMES_price_RT = Foundation_RT × property_multiplier

Example Firenze · 20/05/2026 (property multiplier = ×0.767):

  • Camera Matrim. Deluxe: €318 × 0.767 = €244
  • Suite: (€318 + €15) × 0.767 = €333 × 0.767 = €255
  • Suite with Terrace: (€318 + €70) × 0.767 = €388 × 0.767 = €297

Why this asymmetry? Non-base RTs have little data. A standalone Foundation wouldn't work (LY median unstable on 1-2 units). Per-RT factors would be noisy (one cancellation swings the pace by ±50%). At property level the signals are stable.

Room Type filter (coloured pills)

  • "All" = property aggregate (default). The columns show the totals.
  • One pill per RT. When you select one, the whole table re-colours for that RT (the OTB/Pickup/STLY numbers stay property-level, but the Foundation/RMES price becomes RT-specific).

Foundation override (baseRT only)

Overrides are done only on the baseRT; the other RTs automatically inherit Foundation + supplement. The baseRT Foundation cell has 2 states:

  • ⚡ Computed (default) = the dynamic 6-step Foundation, changes when the data changes.
  • 🖋 Override = a manual value you entered. The 5 RMES factors still react on top of this value.

Two ways to set it:

  • Single (one day): click the baseRT Foundation cell → enter the value. Use ↺ to return to computed.
  • By period (multiple days): in the panel above the table, section Period override, choose From / To, a price €, and apply with 🖋 Foundation (forces the base, RMES reacts on top) or 🔒 Final price (forces the published price, RMES ignored for those days). ↺ Reset period removes both over the range. Everything acts on the baseRT; the other RTs inherit the value + supplement.

Two distinct modals: Foundation and RMES

The calculation detail is split by column, so each cell opens only what concerns it:

  • Click the Foundation cell → detail of Foundation only (the 6 steps: historical anchor, target revenue, expected RN, market cap, floor + base lift). From here you also set the baseRT Foundation override.
  • Click the RMES cell → detail of RMES only: the starting Foundation value (number, without its 6 steps), then the 5 factors one by one with the numbers, the composite multiplier and the suggested price. Here you also enter the final-price override (💾 Save) with live revenue simulation.
  • Important: in the RMES modal the factor numbers (RN, OCC, ADR, pace, searches) are property aggregates, NOT specific to the displayed RT. E.g. "RN 3 / cap 8" = 3 rooms of 8 total for the property.

In practice: every morning open Sell Strategy, scroll 7-14 days, check the days with a green RMES cell (raise) or red (lower). Override only if you know things the system doesn't (events, special situations): single for one day, or by period when you want to fix a price over an interval (e.g. a long weekend, a trade fair) without going day by day.

② CXLTrend rate, reasons and economic impact

What it's for: understand how many bookings get cancelled, by whom (channel/source), when (lead time) and what the revenue impact is.

  • Cancellation rate = cancelled / (confirmed + cancelled). Compared with the equivalent LY period.
  • Cancellation lead time: days between cancellation and check-in. High lead time = early cancellation, low = last-minute (more harmful, less time to re-sell).
  • Top cancelled channels/sources: who cancels the most. Booking.com is usually structurally more cancelled than Direct.
  • Lost revenue: sum of the Total (net of city tax) of the bookings cancelled in the period.

Practical use: if you see an abnormal spike of cancellations in a specific period, investigate (issue with a channel? cancelled event? overly permissive policy?).

③ Overview full year 2026 vs STLY 2025 and Final LY

What it's for: a bird's-eye view of the whole year. It answers "how am I doing overall vs last year?".

  • OTB (On The Books) = bookings confirmed to date for the 2026 period.
  • STLY (Same-Time-Last-Year) = 2025 snapshot at the same day-of-week a year ago (today −364d). A "like-for-like" comparison.
  • Final LY = the definitive 2025 close, all 2025 bookings without time filter. Shows how much value was consolidated last year.

What it contains: monthly pacing (OCC%, RN, ADR, Revenue by month), with columns grouped and visually separated into 5 blocks — 2026 OTB, 2025 STLY, Final LY, Δ vs STLY and Δ vs Final LY — each with a colour band and a divider border so you can read at a glance which group each column belongs to. Plus monthly revenue and OCC charts, Source and Channel tables filtered by month, booking window (lead-time distribution, rolling 12 closed months). Note on Source: when the Provenienza field is empty, the source is inferred from the channel — Booking/Expedia → OTA, Beddy → direct (Sito web) — so OTA vs direct stays correct even on rows without an explicit source.

Quick read: Δ% OTB vs STLY = speed signal (am I selling faster/slower?). Δ% OTB vs Final LY = % of close reached (how much pickup still expected).

④ Forecast closing projection for future months

What it's for: estimate where each future month will close (RN, ADR, Revenue, OCC%), so you know in advance which months are on track and which aren't.

Calculation logic:

  1. OTB to date for the month (RN already confirmed × average ADR).
  2. Expected remaining pickup: how much pickup is expected from now to month-end, based on the historical pattern for the same period last year. "Share by month" calculation = which fraction of the final total had been collected at -X days from the month.
  3. RMES factor: the remaining pickup is not sold at historical ADR; it is sold at the average price RMES is suggesting for those days (weighted by remaining inventory: sold-out days don't count).
  4. Forecast = OTB + (expected_pickup × weighted_avg_RMES_ADR).

Things to know:

  • The "share by month" is recomputed on the LY data of the individual month (not just global): if August historically collects 90% of the total already at -30d, the share is 0.90 for August, not the 0.50 annual average.
  • The expected-pickup ADR uses the month's average RMES price, weighted by remaining inventory for each day (sold-out days → weight 0).
  • Projected closing range + OTB/STLY bars by stay month.

1st-of-month snapshot (Δ% vs snap)

On the 1st day of each month the system freezes that month's forecast (initial snapshot). From then on the snapshot no longer changes. The Δ% vs snap column compares the snapshot with how the month is actually going:

  • Current month and past months: snapshot vs realized OTB (for the current month it's the OTB accumulating day by day; for closed months it's the actual close). It measures how well the initial forecast matches reality.
  • Still-future months: with no significant OTB yet, the comparison is against the live forecast (stays ≈0% until you recompute).

Example: the forecast saved on 1/5 for May says €65,860. During May you compare this number with the incoming OTB; at the end of May (when it becomes a past month) you see snapshot vs actual close. On 1/6 June is frozen, and so on. Positive Δ% = you're closing better than forecast at the start of the month.

⑤ Pickup Matrix stay-month × booking-window matrix

What it's for: visualize in a grid how many RN were sold (or cancelled) for each combination of stay month × booking window. It answers "how many bookings did I take in the last 7 days for August?".

  • Rows: booking windows (last 7d, last 14d, etc.) or by specific day.
  • Columns: stay months (Jan-Dec).
  • Cells: net ΔRN (new - cancelled), average ADR of the new ones.

Practical use: trend spotting. If you see a month column all green (steady positive pickup) you know that month is doing well. A row all red = a week of abnormal cancellations.

⑥ Rate Shopper my price vs competitors on Expedia

What it's for: see, for every future day, my Expedia price vs the competitors', and understand where I'm positioned (above/below the average).

Things to know:

  • Mine: the first row is MY Expedia listing. Conversion to Beddy_eq: gross_price / (1 + Expedia_markup/100) / 0.90. The markup is not a fixed %: it's configurable per channel in tab RMES → Ⓑ (one markup per channel: Expedia/other, Booking, Airbnb/VRBO). If the baseRT is sold out, the supplement of the available higher room type is subtracted. The /0.90 removes the non-refundable discount.
  • Compset weights: each competitor has a 0-100% weight configurable in tab RMES → Ⓒ. Weight 100% counts fully, weight 0% excludes from the calculation. The weight is used both by the D · Online Pricing RMES factor and by the Foundation market cap.
  • Offset €: Foundation only. E.g. -10 = "I want to stay €10 below this competitor". The Foundation market reference = Σ(weight × (compset_Beddy_eq + offset)) / Σ(weight).

Reading: each cell shows that day's competitor price (Beddy_eq). Gradient colouring: lighter = cheaper, darker = pricier. Immediate comparison with my row at the top.

⑨ RMES — Revenue Manu&Enis System dynamic pricing system configuration

What it's for: the automatic dynamic-pricing system configuration tab. Here you don't see prices (those are in Sell Strategy), here you set the parameters that determine how RMES computes prices.

Foundation on-demand (faster loading)

To keep the dashboard fast to open, Foundation is not pre-computed for all properties at startup. On load, only the current property (default: Firenze Suite) is computed. The other properties are computed on-demand: automatically the first time you select them, or manually with the ⚡ Compute Foundation button at the top of the Sell Strategy controls.

The button shows ✓ Foundation ready once a property is computed. Selecting "both" computes all properties. This trades a tiny wait when switching property the first time for a much quicker initial open.

How RMES works (summary)

RMES computes the final price of each day × room type with this formula:

RMES_price = Foundation × (1 + Σ weight × deviation)

The multiplier (1 + Σ weight × deviation) is capped at ±total_cap (default ±30%). Each individual deviation is clamped to ±50%. The deviations come from the 5 factors.

The 5 factors (all property-level, not per RT)

  • A · Demand (occ): current-day OCC vs STLY (-364d, same-DOW). dev = OCC_cur / OCC_STLY − 1. Below LY → brake (lower). Above LY → push (raise).
  • B · Demand (Price): current ADR vs STLY, 3-case conditional logic:
    • Case 1 (ADR < LY): recovery, raise. dev = -(ratio - 1) positive.
    • Case 2 (ADR ≥ LY AND OCC ≥ LY): all good, neutral. dev = 0.
    • Case 3 (ADR ≥ LY but OCC < LY): brake softened by target growth. dev = -((ADR/STLY -1) - target_growth/100). Never positive (never raises in Case 3).
  • C · Pace Trend: pickup over the last 4 weeks of the current month vs the same STLY period. The 4 weeks are weighted, giving more weight to the most recent week (W1 10% · W2 20% · W3 30% · W4 40%, where W4 is the latest week). The ratio uses the weighted pickup; the room-night counts shown elsewhere are the real (unweighted) ones. Fallback hierarchy:
    1. Property 4w month pace. 2. Property-aggregate 4w month pace (badge P̄). 3. Property annual pace only if |dev| ≤ 15%. 4. Neutralized (weight redistributed).
  • D · Online Pricing: my Expedia price vs compset average (weighted by competitor weights). Inverted logic: dev = -((my_Expedia / avg_compset) − 1). If I'm cheaper → positive dev → I can raise.
  • E · Demand (Expedia): that day's Expedia searches vs the month median (not vs the annual average). dev = (search_cur − month_median) / month_median. Search peaks = push, troughs = brake.

RMES modal structure (clicking the price cell)

The detail modal is organized in sections, top to bottom:

  • Foundation Price: the structural starting price (source of the calculation).
  • Market Factors: the 5 factors above, each with weight and deviation % and the expandable calculation detail below. The composite multiplier (weighted sum, limited by the total cap) is shown as a total under the table.
  • Customizations: the Last Minute Price Factor applied that day (see below).
  • Thresholds: Floor rate (minimum limit), Base rate and the property's Cap ±.
  • Restrictions: the suggested minimum stay (MLOS 1 or 2 nights).
  • RMES: the final suggested price.

⏱ Last Minute Price Factor

A customization that applies a discount or premium % to the final RMES price (after the 5 factors, before the floor) based on two dimensions: the property's occupancy on that specific day and the days remaining to arrival. It's a matrix configurable per property in the RMES tab: rows are occupancy thresholds (≤10% … ≤100%), columns are days-to-arrival bands (0-10, 11-30, 31-60, 61-365). Typical logic: low occupancy + imminent arrival → discount (fill last-minute); high occupancy + distant arrival → premium (raise when there's already demand). Negative values = discount, positive = premium.

The 6 Foundation steps

Foundation is the structural price of the day, the source of RMES (no longer the old Beddy→Expedia→Compset→OTB→LY cascade, which is now just a fallback). Computed for the baseRT in 6 steps:

  1. Historical anchor: median LY ADR same-DOW same-month (Beddy_eq).
  2. Target Revenue: anchor × monthly target growth (default +5%, configurable in Ⓐ).
  3. Expected RN at close: estimate of how many nights will sell in total at close = typical LY final fill (median RN sold/day same-DOW same-month) × pace of this year, never less than the OTB already acquired and never above inventory. Expected pickup is the difference (expected RN − OTB). The residual target revenue is divided over these remaining nights → target closing ADR. (Note: "residual LY pickup" is no longer used, as it was distorted when LY closed full — if last year was already full and this year you're empty, you still have to sell.)
  4. Booking window + time curve: adjusted P_raw.
  5. Compset market cap: caps the structural price within a band around the weighted competitor average (with offset). The band is time-aware based on days before check-in:
    • ≤ 60 days before check-in (short term): conservative band [×0.80, ×0.90]. The price is never allowed above 90% of the compset average — we deliberately stay below the market to drive short-term pickup.
    • ≥ 61 days before check-in (medium/long term): normal band [×0.80, ×1.20], as before.
    The floor (×0.80) is the same in both cases; only the upper bound changes. Note: the cap only limits the price — if the computed price is already inside the band, it is left unchanged.
  6. Floor (hard) + Base lift (soft): absolute limits. Configurable in Ⓑ.

Far mode (>180d): simplified = max(Base, LY_median) × growth.

Configurable sections in the RMES tab

  • ① 5-factor weights (default 20% × 5 = 100%). Reset 20%×5 buttons for the current property or for all properties.
  • ② RMES total cap (default ±30%). Limits how far the final price can deviate from Foundation, even if all factors point the same way.
  • Ⓐ Target growth by month (default +5% × 12). Revenue growth vs LY for each month.
  • Ⓑ Base / Floor / Channel markup / Elasticity. Base = annual fair price (default Firenze 220, Condotta 280, Alfani 270, David's 145). Floor = absolute limit below which the price never drops (default per property: Condotta 200, Alfani 200, Firenze 150, David's 100). It applies both to Foundation and to the final RMES price: even if the 5 factors would push lower, the suggested price is locked at the floor. (A manual override can still go wherever you want.) Channel markup (3 configurable types, used both for Expedia positioning and for the per-channel sales history): Expedia/other 17% (Expedia and any channel not specified below), Booking 13%, Airbnb/VRBO 10%. The percentages are not fixed: editable here. Elasticity = RN ↔ price sensitivity (default 1.0).
  • Ⓒ Compset weights and offsets. Which competitors to include, with what weight (0-100%) and what offset (€). Weight and offset apply both to the D · Online Pricing factor of RMES and to the Foundation market cap (same shared configuration). The default values (weights and offsets for the 4 properties) are preloaded; they remain editable here.
  • Ⓓ Post-close override audit. For each closed day with an active override, comparison of override vs snapshot RMES price. Δ Revenue shows which would have done better (assuming the same RN).
  • Ⓔ RT supplements by month (read-only). 12-month × N-RT table with the supplements the system automatically applies to Foundation for non-base RTs. Derived from history, split by high/low season.

Storage: the whole configuration is saved in the browser's localStorage, separate per property. Changing the property in the global selector also changes the property you're configuring in the RMES tab.

📚 Glossary terms and acronyms
ADR
Average Daily Rate = Revenue / RN. Average price per room sold.
Beddy_eq
Beddy-equivalent rate = price net of the OTA markup. Used for like-for-like comparisons. Expedia → Beddy_eq conversion: Expedia_gross / (1 + markup) / 0.90. The markup is not fixed: it is the configurable "Expedia/other" markup in Ⓑ (default 17%); the /0.90 removes the non-refundable discount. If the baseRT is sold out that day, the supplement of the first available higher room is also subtracted (the Expedia price seen is not for the base type).
baseRT
The main Room Type of a property (the one with the most units, the most historical data): Firenze → Camera Matrim. Deluxe, Condotta → Bilocale, Alfani → Classic. It is the only RT for which Foundation is computed from the 6 steps; the others inherit via the monthly supplement.
Compset
Competitive set = the group of direct competitors on Expedia. Weighted average: Σ(weight × (compset_Beddy_eq + offset)) / Σ(weight). Weights and offsets configurable in Ⓒ.
Final LY (Last Year)
The definitive 2025 close = all 2025 bookings without time filter. Shows how much value was consolidated last year.
Foundation
The structural price of the day (the source of RMES). Computed for the baseRT in 6 steps; inherited by non-base RTs via Foundation_baseRT + month_supplement. 2 states: ⚡ computed (dynamic, default) or 🖋 manual override.
MLOS
Minimum Length Of Stay = the "sell only stays of at least N nights" restriction. Suggested by RMES as M1, M2, etc. inside the price cell.
OTB
On The Books = bookings confirmed to date for a future period.
Pace
Pickup speed = how many bookings are coming in over a period. Pace cur vs STLY indicates whether you're selling faster or slower than last year.
Pickup
New bookings acquired over a period (e.g. last 7 days). Net = new - cancelled.
Revenue
Always net of city tax. Stay-night allocation: rev/night = Total / num_nights.
RMES
Revenue Manu&Enis System. The automatic pricing system of this dashboard.
RN
Room Nights = rooms × nights. E.g. 2 rooms for 3 nights = 6 RN.
STLY
Same-Time-Last-Year = last year's snapshot at the same point in the cycle. Day-of-week comparison (today - 364d), not by date. E.g. if today is Friday 20/05/2026, STLY is Friday 21/05/2025.
RT supplement
Historical ADR difference between an RT and the baseRT, split by high/low season, rounded to the nearest multiple of 5. E.g. Suite with Terrace Firenze May = +€70 (high), +€50 (low). Full table in tab RMES → Ⓔ.
